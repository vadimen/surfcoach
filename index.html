01 — Coaching

How I teach

I'm detail-focused and always honest about your progress. Every student I've coached has gained solid kite control and stood up on the board.

01

Theory that sticks

I explain every concept as many times as it takes, until it's genuinely clear to you.

02

Shown by example

I demonstrate each exercise myself first, so you know exactly what it should look like.

03

Practice with feedback

You spend most of the time on the water while I watch and correct as you go.

03 — Feedback

Student reviews

Words from people I have taught, taken from Google reviews.

I had my beginner kitesurfing course with Vadim, and I couldn't have asked for a better instructor! From the very beginning, he was open, friendly, and genuinely invested in my progress. We quickly built a good connection, and thanks to his unique and easy-to-understand teaching style, I was able to grasp the concepts much faster than I expected.

By the third hour of training, I managed to ride my board for my first 5 meters — something I never thought I'd achieve so quickly. This progress would not have been possible without Vadim's dedication. He spent the entire session standing in cold water right next to me, providing constant guidance, support, and encouragement.

After the 4-hour course, I felt that I had gained a solid foundation to continue kitesurfing on my own. However, there are still some advanced techniques and details I'd like to master, and I will definitely book additional lessons with Vadim in the future.

For comparison, a friend of mine took a beginner kitesurfing course at another school and had a completely different experience. He received some basic theory and was then left alone in the water, where he spent most of the time struggling with tangled lines and learned very little.

I highly recommend Fansurf, and especially instructor Vadim. If you're looking for professional coaching, personal attention, and real progress, this is the place to go!
